Our Story

Our business started when one day, two business partners were suddenly craving pastries and sweets. Then, a thought crosses their minds: "What if we sold pastries and sweets with reasonable prices?" Hence, Florivy Bakes is born! We make and sell an array of sweets—from cookies to crinkles with a multiple burst of flavors. Enjoy our decadent and heartwarming baked goods because Florivy bakes for the smiles on your faces!
